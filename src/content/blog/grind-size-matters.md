If a cup tastes sour, it's usually under-extracted — grind finer. If it tastes bitter and flat, it's usually over-extracted — grind coarser. Before you blame the beans, check the grind.

## A quick reference

- **Too fine** for your method → over-extraction, bitterness
- **Too coarse** for your method → under-extraction, sourness

Small adjustments go a long way. Change one click on your grinder at a time and taste again before making another change.

## Why extraction is really about time and surface area

Grind size is, at its core, a proxy for surface area. Finer grounds expose more surface area to water in the same amount of time, so more gets extracted, faster. That's why a fine grind paired with a long brew time (like a French press left too long) reliably turns bitter — the water has had far more surface area, for far more time, than it needs.

The inverse is just as real. A coarse grind in a fast-brewing method, like a 2-minute pour-over, often doesn't have enough contact time to pull out the sugars and acids that make a cup taste balanced — hence the sourness.

## Matching grind to method

Every brew method has an expected contact time, and the "right" grind size is just whatever matches it:

- **Espresso** (25–30 seconds): very fine, almost powder-like
- **Pour-over** (2.5–4 minutes): medium, like table salt
- **French press** (4 minutes): coarse, like breadcrumbs
- **Cold brew** (12–24 hours): extra coarse, since the long steep time needs less surface area to reach full extraction

## A cheap grinder trick

If you only own a blade grinder rather than a burr grinder, pulse it in short bursts and shake the grinder between pulses. It won't match a burr grinder's consistency, but it noticeably reduces the fine "dust" that causes over-extraction in the parts of the grind that are too small.

## The takeaway

Before adjusting your ratio, your water temperature, or switching beans entirely, check the grind first. It's the free variable — no new equipment, no new beans, just a dial you can turn immediately and taste the difference within minutes.
